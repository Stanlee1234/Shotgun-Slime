 Sammy the Slime has fell from the Surface with his Shotgun... Use your shotgun to bounce back towards the surface!

### Controls

Left mouse button (LMB) or Right mouse button (RMB) to launch yourself!

### Inspiration

Watching many videos on Foddian games including "Get to Work", "Getting over It", and "Shotgun Knight", I wanted to create one for the hackathon. My first idea was to create a shooter game about a shotgun (idk why cuz its not even fitting of Foddian) but I found the mechanics of Shotgun Recoil a very good idea for a game. That's where I got my idea. A slime climbing out of a deep cave to return back to the surface.

### Theme

In my game, the theme, "Bounce Back" is incorporated in 2 different ways. The first is the recoil, where you bounce backwards when shooting to launch yourself up the difficult platformer course. You can't move in any other way, so making precise jumps is key.
<img width="260" height="309" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-15 at 12 12 40" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9bba041d-bdae-4b2f-b4bb-501617ec5c49" />

The second way is failure. In my game, whenever you shoot, you eject a shell out near where you shot at. The more you fail, the more shells stack up on the platforms. It turns tough jumps into a actuall visible record, of everytime you have failed at it and "Bounced Back" from.
